Yogācārabhūmi corpus map 瑜伽師地論

Where is each part of the Yogācārabhūmi attested, and in which witness? Every bar is placed on the running text of Xuánzàng's complete Chinese translation (T1579, Taishō vol. 30, 279a–882a). Bibliographic spans follow Martin Delhey's survey (HOS 75, 2013), with his page cited for each claim. Hatched bars are the data-derived layer: the stretches of T1579 to which DharmaNexus e-texts are sentence-aligned.

What is shown, sources and limits

Axis. Taishō page, register and line of T1579 (e.g. 328c7), as numbered in CBETA / DharmaNexus ZH_T30_1579. The three bands above the rows are the five parts, the seventeen bhūmis (and the chapters of the Viniścayasaṃgrahaṇī and Vastusaṃgrahaṇī), and, when zoomed in, sub-sections (the vidyāsthānas of the Śrutamayī Bhūmi, the verse sets of the Cintāmayī Bhūmi, the yogasthānas of the Śrāvakabhūmi, the paṭalas of the Bodhisattvabhūmi). Boundaries are the section headings of T1579.

Bibliographic layer (solid bars). Every span reproduces a statement in Delhey 2013 (pp. 498–561); the tooltip gives the page. Delhey usually locates passages in the coordinates of the edition he cites. They were placed on the T1579 axis as follows (tooltip “placement”): T1579 given by Delhey – used as is; section – Delhey names a bhūmi, paṭala or section, mapped to its T1579 heading; concordance – pages of Bhattacharya 1957 (Bh.), Shukla 1973 (Sh.) or Dutt 1966 (BoBhD) converted with a page concordance computed from the sentence alignments of the DharmaNexus e-texts of these editions (accuracy roughly ±10 lines); Q interpolated – only Peking folios given, interpolated between Delhey's own Q = T equations. Bars with a dashed outline have an uncertain start or end, or are only located by Delhey at section level although they cover just part of it.

Data-derived layer (hatched bars). Contiguous stretches of T1579 to which a DharmaNexus e-text is sentence-aligned (Dharmamitra multilingual alignments). Gaps can mean missing text, or simply passages the aligner did not match (commentaries align only sporadically). The Chinese BoBh translations T1581/T1582/T1500/T1501 are placed through their alignments with the Sanskrit BoBh; the two Wogihara e-texts, which are not aligned, through text matching with the Dutt e-text. Regenerate with scripts/build_data.py.

Limits. Delhey covers publications up to c. 2010; later editions (e.g. further Śrāvakabhūmi Study Group volumes) are not included. Spans of the data-derived layer are automatic and not checked by hand. The bibliographic layer lists only what Delhey locates; passages he mentions without location (e.g. most of the Xiǎnyáng lùn parallels, Tibetan Dūnhuáng fragments) are not drawn.
